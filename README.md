# System Error

A 2D space adventure made for **KiwiHacks Nova 2026**.

## How to Play

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project in **Godot 4.7**.
3. Press **F5** to run the game.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| ← → | Move left / right |
| ↑ ↓ | Move forward / backward |

## Gameplay

The game starts with a screen showing **three portals**. Choose one to begin.

### 🟣 Purple Portal — Outer Space
You are transported to outer space, where danger comes from every direction:
- **Dodge comets** hurtling through the void
- **Fight off monsters** that lurk between the stars
- **Collect iron** to progress and survive

### 🔵 Cyan Portal — The Cyan World
A surreal world filled with cyan trees. Explore and uncover its secrets.

### 🔴 Red Portal — The Red World
Face **Mars monsters** in a hostile red dimension.

Each portal leads to a unique challenge. Only the brave will make it through all three.

## Story

You play as an astronaut stranded in a strange, unfamiliar world. Three mysterious portals stand before you — each one leads to a different dimension. Your mission: survive the challenges, defeat what lurks inside, and find your way home.

## Features

- 🌌 Three unique worlds, each with its own visual style
- ☄️ Dodge comets in outer space
- 👾 Enemies to fight
- ⛏️ Resource collection (iron)
- 🔊 Custom sound effects and music
- 🎨 Custom pixel art and animations
